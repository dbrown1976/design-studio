# Prototyping rules

## Fidelity stages

Use `baseline` for a trusted starting point, `exploration` for divergent
structural bets, and `hifi` for a converged product-fidelity direction.

Low fidelity should communicate that decisions remain open. High fidelity should
be earned through convergence, not used to disguise uncertainty.

## Blueprints

A blueprint is a trusted reference to an existing product surface or pattern.
A prototype may build from a blueprint while explicitly changing a subset of its
behaviour. Record the relationship in `prototype.json`.

## Executable over illustrative

Prefer a functioning interaction to a static representation whenever the
behaviour matters to the design decision.

## Handoff

Keep important implementation intent close to the prototype: what must be
preserved, what can be ignored, and what the prototype is deliberately not
redesigning.
